SPACE SHOOTER LEVEL DESIGN
=========================

ENEMY TYPES
-----------
1. Scour - Basic cannon-fodder ship
2. Fighter - Faster, more aggressive attacker
3. Bomber - Slow, high-health, drops explosive shots
4. Support Ship - Heals or buffs nearby enemies
5. Torpedo Ship - Long-range heavy projectile attacks
6. Frigate - Mini-boss class vessel
7. Battlecruiser - Heavy capital ship
8. Dreadnought - End-of-level boss

LEVEL 1: FIRST CONTACT
----------------------
Wave 1: 10 Scours
Wave 2: 10 Scours
Wave 3: 8 Scours, 2 Fighters
Wave 4: 6 Scours, 4 Fighters
Wave 5: 10 Fighters
Wave 6: 8 Fighters, 2 Bombers
Wave 7: 6 Fighters, 4 Bombers
Wave 8: 10 Bombers
Wave 9: 8 Bombers, 2 Support Ships
Wave 10: 6 Bombers, 4 Support Ships
Wave 11: 10 Support Ships
Wave 12: Frigate (Mini Boss)

LEVEL 2: ESCALATION
-------------------
Wave 13: 6 Fighters, 4 Support Ships
Wave 14: 8 Fighters, 2 Torpedo Ships
Wave 15: 6 Fighters, 4 Torpedo Ships
Wave 16: 10 Torpedo Ships
Wave 17: 4 Bombers, 4 Support Ships, 2 Torpedo Ships
Wave 18: 8 Bombers, 4 Torpedo Ships
Wave 19: 12 Mixed Fighters
Wave 20: 2 Frigates

LEVEL 3: FLEET ACTION
---------------------
Wave 21: 12 Fighters
Wave 22: 8 Fighters, 4 Bombers
Wave 23: 6 Bombers, 4 Support Ships
Wave 24: 6 Torpedo Ships, 6 Fighters
Wave 25: 4 Frigates, 8 Fighters
Wave 26: 2 Frigates, 6 Bombers, 6 Fighters
Wave 27: Battlecruiser with 8 Fighter Escorts

LEVEL 4: CAPITAL SHIP OFFENSIVE
-------------------------------
Wave 28: 2 Battlecruisers
Wave 29: Battlecruiser, 2 Frigates, 8 Fighters
Wave 30: 2 Battlecruisers, 4 Torpedo Ships
Wave 31: 3 Frigates, 6 Support Ships
Wave 32: Battlecruiser, 12 Mixed Escorts
Wave 33: 3 Battlecruisers

FINAL ASSAULT
-------------
Wave 34: 2 Battlecruisers, 4 Frigates
Wave 35: 4 Frigates, 8 Torpedo Ships
Wave 36: Battlecruiser, 2 Frigates, 12 Fighters
Wave 37: 2 Battlecruisers, 12 Mixed Ships
Wave 38: Dreadnought Phase 1
Wave 39: Dreadnought Phase 2 (launches Fighters)
Wave 40: Dreadnought Phase 3 (launches Bombers and uses Super Weapon)

SPAWN RULES
-----------
A wave must be completely defeated or all enemies must pass the player before the next wave spawns.

Wave Starts
 -> Enemies Spawn
 -> Player Destroys Enemies OR Enemies Leave Screen
 -> Wave Complete
 -> 2 Second Delay
 -> Next Wave Starts
